# A1 — Working with Open Data

**Dataset:** Burke-Gilman Trail counter north of NE 70th St
**Source:** https://data.seattle.gov/d/2z5v-ecg8 (latest update: September 8, 2026)
**Date pulled:** 2026-10-12
**License / terms of use:** Public Domain

## Questions I asked
1. When is the trail busiest, and when is it quietest?
2. Do cyclists and pedestrians use the trail at different times of day? 
3. Commuting or recreation? Is there evidence people use the Burke-Gilman to commute, not just for fun? 
4. Do people travel in different directions (north vs. south) in the morning and evening?

## How to re-run
Open `A1.ipynb` and run all cells (needs `requests`, `pandas`, `matplotlib`).
